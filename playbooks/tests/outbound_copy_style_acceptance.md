# Outbound copy style acceptance

- [ ] Email, DM/SMS, comment/reply, social/group post, blog, video, notification, report prose, and copy-ready snippets contain no `—`, `&mdash;`, `&#8212;`, or `&#x2014;` in raw or rendered outward text.
- [ ] Comments are one useful point, normally 1-3 short sentences; posts have one idea and CTA; email/DM preserves proof, adaptive richness, and required footer without a universal sentence cap.
- [ ] Blog/video preserve their existing budgets, structure, credibility, and required disclaimers without padding.
- [ ] Source/evidence text is retained internally; an em-dash source line is paraphrased or omitted outward, never silently changed in evidence.
- [ ] Concision retains evidence, caveats, compliance, opt-out/footer, and channel requirements.
- [ ] Writer lint occurs before review/provider handoff and the final send/publish/render gate lints again; any hit is `copy_style_violation` and is rewritten.
- [ ] A post-approval final-gate hit sends, publishes, and creates nothing; revised copy returns to pending approval and receives fresh approval before any action. Approved text is never silently rewritten.
